the final accessibility attachment reports `00:00:05:23`, near the end of the

## AVPlayer

`testNativePreviewSeekScreenshotAndReopen` reached `AVPlayer ready`, sought to
`00:00:01:00`, and successfully enabled screenshot reveal after capturing a frame.
The subsequent synthesized `l` key took approximately 10 seconds, again with
infinite-rectangle display warnings. Playback timecode did not advance from

## Interpretation and remaining work

The slow synthesized key events and display warnings make keyboard delivery or
focus a plausible cause, but do not prove it. MPV's first seek, screenshot, playback,
and pause succeeded, so the run does not demonstrate that those backend operations
are generally broken. Equally, successful initial operations do not establish that
reopening and subsequent playback are reliable.

Repeat both tests under controlled foreground/focus conditions, inspect the actual
text-field value before submitting the second seek, and verify playback with both
keyboard and direct controls before changing production code. Manual playback and
a completely passing seek/screenshot/reopen regression remain release checks.
