# 4.5 trimmed merge audio validation — 2026-09-19

## Scope

The live merge coexistence regression now also exercises two generated all-intra
H.264 MOV clips with mono 48 kHz PCM audio. The clips carry distinct 440 Hz and
880 Hz tones and are each trimmed from 0.5 to 1.5 seconds through the real
`ConversionManager.convertGroup` Stream Copy path and bundled FFmpeg.

As in the video-only case, an agent job queues while merge preparation owns the
execution gate and starts only after both group rows publish the merged output.
The agent produces its own separate planned output. Both outputs fully decode,
contain 48 video frames, and retain mono 48 kHz audio.

Decoded merged PCM is checked for bounded duration, tone order in both clip
interiors, and audible energy in every 10 ms window around the possible join.
The existing video-only regression continues through the same fixture.

## Observed Stream Copy limitation

The initial sample-exact assertion failed: the merged output contains 98,304
samples (2.048 seconds), despite retaining two seconds of video. Input-side
Stream Copy trimming retains whole PCM packets. The regression therefore allows
at most one 1,024-sample packet at each of the four cut edges and measures tone
order away from the rounded boundary. This records the existing behavior; it
does not establish sample-accurate cuts or exact audio/video alignment.

## Limits

This covers generated all-intra video with uncompressed mono audio and automated
decoding/energy checks. It does not prove perceptually seamless joins, compressed
audio encoder-delay handling, long-GOP cuts, multichannel stitching, or in-app
playback. Encoding-time cancellation, specialized post-actions, named-client
workflows, bilingual review, and signed Release packaging remain open gates.
No production code, dependencies, or bundled binaries changed.
