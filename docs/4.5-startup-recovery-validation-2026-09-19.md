# 4.5 startup recovery validation — 2026-09-19

## Scope

Startup restoration awaited registry operations before completing recovery.
Concurrent callers could enter restoration against the same saved snapshot,
replace registry contents again, or mutate plans and submitted-plan links while
another caller was still rebuilding them. Checking `didRestore` alone did not
protect these suspension points.

A dedicated restoration gate now serializes the whole recovery operation.
Persisted-service entry points wait through this gate even when recovery has
marked its in-memory state restored, so they cannot bypass its remaining cleanup
and persistence work. Both success and error paths release the gate. Submission
and execution retain their separate gates. No wire schema, dependencies, or
bundled binaries changed.
