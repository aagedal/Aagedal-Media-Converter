surfaces, but it does not replace bilingual visual review. The inspector still
needs English/Norwegian layout and clipping inspection with representative long
paths, comments, routing descriptions, and per-source destinations.

disconnect/reconnect, and restart checks; revoked and unavailable-volume access;
real conversion/playback and channel-layout checks for all six presets; the

nested-code verification, notarization, stapling, and clean-machine installation.

cases also remain on the legacy executor and need an explicit coexistence decision.
