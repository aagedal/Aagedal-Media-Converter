# 4.5 accepted-plan retention validation — 2026-09-19

## Scope

Retention previously removed all plans after 15 minutes, including accepted
plans. This discarded same-plan retry links and made queue inspection regenerate
output names from the request. Date-sensitive naming can change after a timezone
change, so regeneration is not equivalent to retaining the accepted mapping.

Accepted plans and their submission links now remain for the lifetime of their
job. Unsubmitted plans retain the 15-minute expiry. Removing a terminal record
at the 30-day retention boundary removes its associated plans and links too.
Active records are never removed by terminal-record retention.

Older snapshots whose plans were already removed still use the existing request
fallback; this change cannot recover discarded output mappings. No schema, UI,
dependency, or bundled binary changed.
