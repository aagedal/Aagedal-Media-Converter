# 4.5 job-persistence validation — 2026-09-13

## Scope

- Continued milestone 2 after conversion planning and output reservation.
- Added a versioned JSON snapshot for plans, submitted-plan links, job records,
  terminal results, and requester-scoped idempotency identities.
- Published the snapshot atomically under the app's Application Support folder.
- Restored job order and idempotency ownership before accepting new persisted
  work, including retries made through a newly created equivalent plan.
- Marked restored queued, running, and cancelling jobs interrupted rather than
  restarting them after an app relaunch.
- Established a 15-minute plan lifetime and 30-day terminal-record/idempotency
  retention window. Active jobs are never removed by retention cleanup.
- Rejected damaged, unsupported, or internally inconsistent snapshots and kept
  their files intact for diagnosis rather than replacing them with empty state.
- Wired restoration into app startup. The service also gates reads and mutations on
  successful restoration, preventing a startup race from overwriting old state.
