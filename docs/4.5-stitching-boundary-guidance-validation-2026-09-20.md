# 4.5 stitching boundary guidance — 2026-09-20

## Change

The stitching editor displays a persistent, wrapping notice below the timeline
when the group's effective preset is Stream Copy. It explains that exported cuts
are approximate and can include extra video frames and audio even at keyframes.
It also explains that preview and timeline duration represent the requested
selection, while exported boundaries and duration can differ.

The notice replaces the information icon's tooltip, which only said cut points
may depend on source keyframes. English and Norwegian Bokmål are included. The
notice has the accessibility identifier `stitching.streamCopyBoundaryGuidance`.
Other presets do not display it.

This addresses the user-visible guidance option left open by the
[long-GOP regression findings](4.5-stitching-long-gop-validation-2026-09-20.md).
The export pipeline and its characterized packet-copy behavior are unchanged.
No exact-cut guarantee or keyframe snapping is introduced.
