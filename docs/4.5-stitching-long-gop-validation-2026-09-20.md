# 4.5 long-GOP stitching validation — 2026-09-20

## Scope and finding

Two live regressions exercise `ConversionManager.convertGroup`, bundled FFmpeg,
and a waiting shared-service agent job with marker export enabled and disabled.
Generated sources contain two seconds of 24 fps H.264 video with closed 12-frame
GOPs, two fixed B-frames, disabled scene-cut keyframes, and stereo 48 kHz AAC.
The clips have distinct hues and distinct tones in each channel. Both retain the
requested 0.5–1.5 second range, whose edges fall on keyframes.

The initial exact-trim assertions failed. Each prepared clip retained 26 video
frames rather than 24: source frames 12 through 36 inclusive, followed by the
forward reference frame 39. The merged output contained 52 frames, and decoded
audio contained 106,496 sample frames instead of the requested 96,000. Measured
container duration was approximately 2.2874 seconds without markers and 2.2183
seconds with markers. The second chapter began at approximately 1.1093 seconds.

This is an unresolved Stream Copy timing limitation, even with keyframe-aligned
cuts. No production fix or exact long-GOP trimming support is claimed. The tests
explicitly characterize retained reference-frame tails; they must be revised if
trim preparation changes to remove those tails. They are not an exact-trim
acceptance test.

## Regression assertions

- Independently decode each original source from its beginning and select the
  observed source frame indices. Every byte of the merged decoded YUV output
  must match those frames in clip order, including the two reference-frame tails.
- Require exactly 52 merged video frames and 48 frames in the untrimmed agent
  output, correct stereo/sample-rate metadata, and error-free decoding.
- Bound container duration by the requested two seconds plus AAC packet rounding
  and B-frame tail/timestamp-normalization allowance.
- Bound decoded audio length by AAC packet rounding plus two video-frame
  intervals per clip. Verify both channel tones in each clip and audible energy
  in every 10 ms window from 0.95 to 1.21 seconds across the extended join.
- Verify cut chapter labels, bounded second-chapter timing, final chapter end
  matching measured duration, and EDL labels when markers are enabled.
- Require the agent to remain queued until the manual merge has published, then
  finish successfully with a separate planned output.
