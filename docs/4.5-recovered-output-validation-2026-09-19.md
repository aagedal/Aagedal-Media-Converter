# 4.5 recovered output validation — 2026-09-19

## Scope

Startup recovery previously validated persisted plans and submission links but
trusted job result URLs independently. A damaged snapshot could therefore expose
unrelated or reordered output files through the queue and job lookup.

Recovery now compares saved outputs with the retained original accepted plan.
Successful jobs require every accepted output in order; other states can retain
an ordered prefix, and queued jobs must have no outputs. Invalid records reject
the entire snapshot before registry mutation or queue publication. The snapshot
remains untouched, allowing the same service to retry after repair.

Equivalent idempotent retry plans do not replace the original filenames. Older
snapshots without a retained original plan keep their existing recovery behavior;
recovery does not recompute date-dependent filenames or require files to remain
on disk. No schema, dependency, binary, or UI changes were made.
