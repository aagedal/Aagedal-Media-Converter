# Output entry safety — 2026-09-22

Shared-job output reservations now respect the authorized destination volume's
case sensitivity. Case variants in a batch are rejected as duplicate outputs on
case-insensitive volumes; competing jobs cannot reserve those variants together.
Case-sensitive volumes retain distinct names. Unknown volume capabilities use
conservative case-insensitive identity. The accepted output paths stay unchanged,
and cancellation releases the captured reservation.

Destination entry checks now recognize dangling symbolic links. Planning warns
about them, and submission/execution reject the occupied output instead of treating
a missing link target as an available filename. Neither the link nor its target
is modified by validation.

## Limits

These checks do not claim atomic no-replace publication against other processes,
hard-link identity, directory retargeting after admission, physical drive removal,
or OS-level grant revocation. No bundled binary or dependency changed.
