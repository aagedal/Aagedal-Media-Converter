# 4.5 atomic recovery validation — 2026-09-19

## Scope

Startup recovery previously replaced the job registry before checking persisted
plans and submitted-plan links. A later validation failure left partially
restored jobs visible to new queue observers even though service requests failed.

Recovery now validates plans and links in local collections before restoring the
registry. The registry's existing atomic record/idempotency validation is the
last validation step before committing service state. Invalid snapshots remain
untouched, and the same service can retry after the stored snapshot is repaired.
No wire schema, dependencies, or bundled binaries changed.

## Validation

- The new regression failed before the fix because invalid snapshots exposed
  queued records through `recordUpdates()`.
- Debug build and all 80 `ApplicationJobContractTests` passed after the fix,
  with zero failures or skipped tests.
- `git diff --check` passed.

The regression covers unsupported plan schemas, duplicate plan IDs, missing
submitted plans, missing submitted jobs, and duplicate submitted-plan links.
For each case it attempts recovery twice, checks the exact error, verifies an
empty visible queue and unchanged snapshot bytes, repairs the snapshot, and
verifies recovery plus idempotent submission on the same service instance.
Recovered work is interrupted and retains its original job identity.
