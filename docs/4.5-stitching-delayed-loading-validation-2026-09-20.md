# 4.5 stitching delayed-loading continuation — 2026-09-20

## Changes

The sequence preview preserves its requested source position while paused.
Previously, a paused MPV startup callback reporting zero could replace the
pending trimmed in-point before readiness triggered the final seek. Readiness
also now checks that the preview is active and still owns the prepared item.

Two UI regressions delay preparation of the second generated source by five
seconds. Both native and MPV cases pause during that transition, check the
player's reported clock at the two-second source in-point, resume through the
sequence end, and close/reopen the editor during another delayed transition.
The replacement editor is observed beyond the abandoned preparation's delay.
The delay task is cancelled by the controller's existing teardown path.

The delay and clock display require a Debug UI test session; the delay additionally
requires its explicit launch flag and the generated second fixture's filename.
They are absent from Release builds. The backend setup path remains shared with
ordinary preview preparation.

Seventeen missing Norwegian translations for the recently added timeline marker
and waveform controls are filled in, including help text and export settings.

## Limits

This models a slow preparation step, not a disconnected drive or a decoder that
hangs after initialization. Real memory-card, MXF, long-recording, OS-level access
loss, and reordered/trimmed export checks remain open. The catalog audit does
not establish bilingual visual layout. No dependencies or media binaries changed.
