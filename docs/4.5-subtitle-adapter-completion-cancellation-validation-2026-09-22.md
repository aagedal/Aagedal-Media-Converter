# Subtitle adapter completion cancellation validation — 2026-09-22

## Change

Whisper transcription and bitmap subtitle extraction now check parent-task cancellation immediately after their FFmpeg runner returns, before flushing buffered progress or interpreting the process exit status. A successful subprocess exit racing cancellation therefore remains cancellation at the adapter boundary. Existing service publication checks continue to protect final subtitle files.

This matches the cancellation check already used by the Tesseract OCR engine. No process ownership, subprocess deadline, output naming, or publication policy changes are included.

## Regression coverage

Two deterministic tests inject a runner that buffers an unterminated FFmpeg progress record, cancels its current task, and returns a successful process result:

- `testWhisperTranscriberRejectsSuccessAfterCancellationBeforeFlushingProgress`
- `testTesseractSubtitleExtractorRejectsSuccessAfterCancellationBeforeFlushingProgress`

Both require `CancellationError` and no progress callback from the buffered record. Without the post-await cancellation checks, both adapters return success and flush the stale record.
