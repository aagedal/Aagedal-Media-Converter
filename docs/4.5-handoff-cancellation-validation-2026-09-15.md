# 4.5 handoff cancellation validation — 2026-09-15

## Scope

A cancellation can reach the shared service while its running transition is
being persisted, before FFmpeg has taken ownership of the job. The service now
reads the authoritative state again at the handoff and completes a cancelling
job without launching the executor. Once handoff starts, the FFmpeg adapter
retains a targeted cancellation delivered before its `execute` call begins,
instead of clearing it at startup. Cancellation for a different active job is
still ignored.

The new regression delivers cancellation before adapter execution and verifies
that the result is cancelled without starting or cancelling an FFmpeg runner.
The existing active-runner test continues to verify cancellation of a conversion
already in progress.
